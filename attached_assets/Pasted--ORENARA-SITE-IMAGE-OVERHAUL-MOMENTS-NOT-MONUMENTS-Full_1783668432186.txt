# ORENARA — SITE IMAGE OVERHAUL: MOMENTS, NOT MONUMENTS
## Full audit + generation prompts. Work ONE image at a time, validate against the checklist, then move to the next.

---

## THE DOCTRINE

People buy the evening, not the extrusion. Every marketing image on the site should read as a **moment** — occupied, warm, alive — with the lighting as the quiet reason the moment works. But generated humans are the highest-risk element in AI imagery, so we use a three-tier strategy:

- **Tier P (People, distant):** small figures, backs to camera, silhouettes, mid-to-far distance, never a face in focus, never more than 4 people, people occupy <20% of frame height. Used where the scene IS social.
- **Tier E (Evidence of life):** no humans; the scene was occupied 30 seconds ago — set table, two wine glasses, cheese platter, chair pushed back, towel on pool edge, book left open, door ajar with interior glow. Zero uncanny risk, full warmth. Used for intimate frames.
- **Tier S (Spec/product):** no people, no props. Documentary or product photography. The proof layer — credibility depends on these staying clinical.

## GLOBAL STYLE BLOCK — prepend to EVERY prompt below

> Photorealistic editorial architectural photography, Australian contemporary home, deep dusk blue hour, warm 2700K LED strip lighting as the primary light source, glow grazing surfaces, deep charcoal shadows, muted cinematic grade, slight haze of evening warmth, shot on 35mm full-frame, f/4, natural composition, no oversaturation, no HDR look, no lens flare, premium and restrained. No text, no watermarks, no logos.

**People clause (append only to Tier P prompts):**
> Any people are small in frame, photographed from behind or in silhouette, softly out of focus, faces never visible or in focus, relaxed natural posture, dressed in smart-casual neutral tones.

**Technical note:** the site applies a darkening overlay and slight desaturation to all imagery. Generate ~10% brighter and warmer than you want the final to appear. Match aspect ratio to the slot (noted per image).

## VALIDATION CHECKLIST — run on every generated image before accepting

1. Hands/faces: no visible malformed hands, no in-focus faces, correct limb counts on every figure (zoom in).
2. The LIGHTING is physically plausible: glow comes from where strip would actually be (toe-kicks, under rails, garden beds, coves) and light falls correctly.
3. No fixtures visible that contradict the product (no bulbs, no lanterns, no spotlights as hero light).
4. Colour temperature: warm amber 2700K glow, dusk-blue sky. Reject anything green, magenta, or cool-white.
5. Architecture reads Australian contemporary (rendered walls, timber, glass balustrade, native-adjacent planting) — not Tuscan villa, not American McMansion.
6. Props are coherent (wine glasses have stems, platters have real food, furniture sits flat).
7. Sits comfortably next to already-approved images in the set — same hour, same grade, same photographer energy.

### DO NOT REPLACE — these stay as-is:
- **Submerged strip ("IP68. SUBMERGED. OPERATING.")** — the proof shot. Clinical is its power.
- **Component/product images** (strip close-up, driver, channel, connectors) — spec layer, Tier S by definition.
- **Mounting system images** (rigid channel vs segmented track) — technical comparison.
- **Trade review photo grid** — presented as installer job-site photos; documentary style IS their authenticity. Adding lifestyle staging to "verified delivery photos" would undermine the review section.
- **OG image / favicon** — wordmark assets.

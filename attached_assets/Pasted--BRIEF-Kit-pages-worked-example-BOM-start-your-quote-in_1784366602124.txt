Heading: **How a system comes together.**

Intro line: Every Orenara system is specced from your measurements. Here's how a typical job builds — an 18-metre garden edge in two runs.

Walkthrough items (render as a stepped or annotated list, quantities prominent):
1. **Two sealed runs — 11m and 7m.** Each run is factory-sealed and cut to your measured length. Corners terminate and restart. No field joins, ever.
2. **Two drivers.** No driver serves more than 12 metres of strip, and a run is never split across drivers. 11m takes one driver; adding the 7m run would exceed 12m, so it gets its own.
3. **Two connector and end-cap sets.** One sealed set per run — the same IP68 rating as the strip itself.
4. **Two mains plugs.** One per driver. Pre-wired, AU/NZ, ready for your electrician.
5. **18 metres of channel.** Rigid for the straight sections, segmented track for the curve — measured to your run lengths.

Closing line: Your job won't be this job. That's the point — every system is specced to your measurements and confirmed on your quote before anything is built.

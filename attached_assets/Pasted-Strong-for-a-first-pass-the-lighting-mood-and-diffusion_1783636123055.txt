Driver has an exposed green terminal block and a cable attached. Also too short and chunky — it lost the slim 30cm linear form.
Dimmer wires exit the side with connector-ish tips rather than plain bare ends. Minor.

Two fallbacks if the plug still won't behave — and it may not, this is one of the hardest objects to force:

Regenerate just that item. If Replit's generator supports inpainting/editing a region, mask the plug and rerun only that with the geometry description. Cheaper than rolling the whole composition and losing this lighting.
Composite the real one. Photograph your actual plug and lead on any dark surface, cut it out, drop it into the render. It's a matte black object on a dark background — the easiest possible composite, and it guarantees the one component where accuracy is legally loaded (RCM-marked mains hardware) is genuinely what ships.

Everything else about this frame is worth keeping. If the next run nails items 1–3 but fumbles the plug again, take the composite route and move on rather than burning generations on it.

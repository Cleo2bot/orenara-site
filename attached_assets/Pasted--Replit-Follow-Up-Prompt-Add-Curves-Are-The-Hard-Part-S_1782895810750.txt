## SECTION: Straight Lines vs. Curves — The Mounting System

**Section eyebrow:** THE MOUNTING SYSTEM
**Headline:** Straight lines are easy. Curves are the hard part.

**Intro line:** Any strip lights up fine in a straight run. The hard part — the part most products get wrong — is a curve that still looks intentional five years in.

### Two mounting systems, same strip:

**1. Rigid Channel — for straight lines**
- Aluminium channel, click-in strip mounting
- For pergola beams, benchtops, straight garden edges — anywhere the line doesn't need to bend
- Zero flex, zero risk of the strip sagging or pulling loose over time

**2. Flexible Segmented Track — for curves**
- This is the part that actually matters. Most "flexible" strip lighting just relies on the silicone sleeve itself bending — no structure, no consistent radius, and it shows: uneven light spacing at the bend, visible strain points, and a strip that slowly works itself loose from whatever clip was holding it.
- Duskline's track is segmented specifically to bend into a clean, consistent curve — garden paths, pool edges, curved pergola lines — and the strip clicks directly into the track. It's mechanically secured into the track, not just resting in a channel and hoping.
- Horizontal bend only. This isn't a garden hose — it won't loop vertically, and we won't tell you it does.

**Closing line:** Pick your mounting system based on the geometry of your space, not the limitations of the product. Straight run: rigid channel. Curve: segmented track. Both take the same strip, same connectors, same IP68 rating end to end.

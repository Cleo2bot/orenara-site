## Before You Publish This

- This draft assumes an Australian consumer/trade sales context under the Australian Consumer Law. If Duskline sells into other jurisdictions (NZ, etc.), those need separate legal review — ACL protections and exclusions don't necessarily translate.
- Clause 5.4 (business exclusions) has specific eligibility requirements under the ACL (there's a monetary threshold and other tests for when a business-to-business supply can validly exclude certain guarantees) — a lawyer needs to confirm Duskline's typical order sizes actually qualify before this clause is relied upon.
- [STATE/TERRITORY] in clause 11.3 needs to be filled in based on where Duskline is legally based/registered.
- Consider whether product packaging or point-of-sale materials also need a short-form safety warning (e.g. "Mains wiring must be carried out by a licensed electrician") independent of these Terms, since a customer may never read the full T&C before installing.

## The Technical Specs (Crucial for 2026)

Google's AI actively scans and categorizes images now. You need to feed it the right signals before you upload:

* **File Naming:** Do not upload `IMG_4892.jpg`. Rename the file on your computer to exactly what the trade searches for: `IP68-LED-strip-wiring-diagram.jpg` or `RCM-compliant-landscape-lighting-kit.jpg`.
* **Format & Size:** Must be JPG or PNG, strictly between 10 KB and 5 MB.
* **Dimensions:** Google's current standard prefers a 4:3 ratio (1200x900) or square (720x720). Keep important text or diagrams away from the edges so they don't get cropped on mobile.

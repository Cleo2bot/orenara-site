## Fix 2 — "Four components" section describes a retired product

This section currently lists a **"Touch Dimmer Panel — wall-mounted, scene presets, wireless pairing"** and a **"240V Mains Lead — AU/NZ plug, pre-wired, plug & play"** as standard kit contents. Both describe the self-install, app-controlled product that was deliberately retired before this build started. Dimming/control is explicitly out of scope for the current catalog. "Plug & play" implies no electrician needed, which contradicts every installation note on every current kit page.

Replace the four components with what's actually shipped:
1. **IP68 Silicone Strip** — dot-free diffusion, fully submersible, sealed end caps, 24V DC
2. **Channel & Mount** — aluminium or stainless, straight or flexible, matched to the application
3. **Factory IP68 Connectors** — moulded onto strip ends, pressure-tested before dispatch
4. **Driver** — Mean Well HLG, IP67, sized to the run

**Confirm before writing mains-connection copy:** every current kit page's installation note says the electrician "connects it to mains" — check whether that means hardwired (no plug) or whether there genuinely is a plug involved. Don't guess; if it's hardwired, drop any plug/GPO language entirely rather than softening it.

## Fix 3 — Kit cards: wrong names, wrong links, and a duplication risk worth avoiding

Current cards: "Pathway Kit," "Pergola Kit," "Pool & Water Feature Kit," "Custom Zone Kit," linking to `/kits/pathway-kit`, `/kits/pergola-kit`, `/kits/pool-water-feature-kit`, `/kits/custom-zone-kit` — none of which match the real slugs (`pool-surround`, `stair`, `patio`, `path`).

**Don't just fix the names and links — simplify this section instead of maintaining a second copy of it.** `/kits` already has correct, current cards with real lengths and live pricing. Keeping a second, separately-written version of the same four kits on the homepage means two places that can drift apart — which is exactly the failure pattern that caused several of the bugs found and fixed this session (two places computing or describing the same thing, one gets updated, the other doesn't).

Replace this section with a shorter teaser — the four category images/icons and a one-line description each is fine — with a single clear CTA: **"See all kits →"** to `/kits`. Let `/kits` be the one source of truth for kit details, not the homepage.

## Fix 4 — "How it works" describes the old manual-only process

Current: Step 1 Enquire (1–2 day response) → Step 2 We confirm spec/pricing/delivery → Step 3 Made to order, shipped. This describes the process for every job, when most jobs now price and confirm instantly.

Replace:
- **Step 1 — Configure online.** Pick your application, size it to your space, see the price straight away.

- **Step 3 — Made to order, shipped to you.** *(keep as-is — still accurate)*

## Fix 6 — Footer and "made to order" section imply self-install

Two lines currently allow self-install:
- "Orenara supplies the system only; installation is arranged separately by you or your electrician."
- Footer: "installation is your responsibility and must be carried out by a licensed electrician **where required** by Australian standards."
